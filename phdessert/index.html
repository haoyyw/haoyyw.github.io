Our story

Made with the discipline of research and the pleasure of dessert.

PhDessert began with a simple idea: careful work can still feel generous. The PhD reference stays quietly in the details, while the desserts remain the centre of attention.

The result is a small Sheffield pâtisserie with a boutique sensibility: fewer products, considered ingredients and a calm visual language.
